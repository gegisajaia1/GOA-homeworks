3 important rules in basketball


  1. Traveling adn Double Dribble: You must bounce the ball with one hand to move Taking more than two steps without
    dribbling is traveling Stopping your dribble and starting again or using two hands at once is a double dribble.
  2. how scoring works: Baskets made inside the arc line are worth 2 points shots from behind
    the arc line are worth 3 points and free throws after a foul are worth 1 point
  3. You cannot hit push or hold or an opponent doing so results in a foul committing 5 fouls disqualifies a player from the game
Pro Tip: Keep your eyes up when dribbling.
